\section{Introduction and Progress Summary}
\label{sec:intro}
Resellers profit from recognizing under-priced archive pieces (Comme des Gar\c{c}ons, Maison
Margiela, Undercover, \dots) in thrift stores, but pricing them takes expert knowledge: value
depends on the house, the sub-line, and above all the \emph{era}, which collectors read from the
garment's label. Our project asks whether a model can do the same from a phone photo: predict brand
and era from the label, then estimate market value from comparable items.
