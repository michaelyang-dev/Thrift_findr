The proposal's first milestone was to fix era definitions per brand. Because the input is a label,
we define eras by \emph{visible label changes} and by the date codes some houses print on care
tags, rather than by fixed seasons (Table~\ref{tab:eras}). The definitions come from collector
guides, museum records (e.g.\ NGV, V\&A) and dated listings, and were fact-checked in a second
pass; each era carries its sources and a confidence rating in
\texttt{configs/brand\_eras.yaml}. We will verify them on physical garments, and merge
neighbouring eras where we cannot collect at least 50 label photos.
